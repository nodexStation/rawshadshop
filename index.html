Our story

RAW-স্বাদ — the taste of raw, done right

Rawshad is a Bangladeshi brand for dry fruit powders & healthy snacks. “স্বাদ” means taste — and our whole idea is that real food already tastes good, so we keep it pure.

Everything we sell is 100% natural, with zero additives and zero added sugar. We show real product photos and real prices wherever they are confirmed, and we confirm every order personally before anything is paid.

Why Rawshad

Honest food, honest shop

Zero Additives

Zero Added Sugar

Frequently asked questions

Add products to the cart and place your order — or simply DM us on Instagram. We confirm your order details with you personally, then you receive it as confirmed in that conversation.

Delivery area, timing and any delivery fee are confirmed with you in DM or email when you order — before you pay anything. If a fixed delivery fee is ever set, it will be shown in the checkout here.

Right now orders are confirmed manually with you first. Cash on delivery, bKash and Nagad are not yet available on this website — when they are offered, they will appear as selectable options in the checkout.

We only publish prices we can stand behind. Beetroot Powder prices are listed on this site. For Thankuni Powder, Dried Mango and Mixed Berry Medley, message us on Instagram and we will reply with the current price.

General guidance: store in a cool, dry place in an airtight container, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Always check the product packaging for specific instructions.

Return and replacement details are confirmed with you at order time. If something arrives wrong or damaged, message us on Instagram with your order reference and we will sort it out with you.
